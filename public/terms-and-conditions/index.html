1. The service

ChatCart Pro, provided by Nexsofture Tech FZ LLC, is an AI ordering assistant that takes orders from your customers on your WhatsApp number and phone line, checks stock in your shop system, and sends orders to your dashboard or kitchen display, with delivery rider management.

2. AI assistant

The assistant is an AI. It can make mistakes, so order summaries are shown to the customer for confirmation. You must not present it as a human, and you must not remove its AI introduction or the call-recording notice. Your staff remain responsible for fulfilling orders correctly.

3. Inbound only

Voice ordering is for inbound calls that customers make to you. You must not use ChatCart Pro for unsolicited outbound sales or cold calls.
