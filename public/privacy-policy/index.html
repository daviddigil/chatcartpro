6. WhatsApp and broadcasts

We use the WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API) provided by Meta. Messages pass through Meta's systems under Meta's own terms. Broadcast and offer messages are sent only to people who opted in, and they can opt out at any time by replying STOP. We follow the WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy.

7. UAE data protection (PDPL) and other laws

We handle personal data in line with the UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (PDPL). Where a business serves customers in India, we also respect applicable Indian data protection law. We process data only for the purposes above, keep it only as long as needed, and do not sell it.
