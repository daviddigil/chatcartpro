How it works

From "I want rice and milk" to a packed order

Customer messages or calls

On your own WhatsApp number or phone line. In Tamil, Hindi, Malayalam, Telugu or English.

The AI takes the order

It understands the list, checks live stock in your shop's system, and suggests alternatives.

Customer confirms

They see items and total in the chat and say yes. No app, no link, no sign-up.

It reaches your team

The order appears on your store dashboard or kitchen display. A rider is assigned if it is a delivery.
