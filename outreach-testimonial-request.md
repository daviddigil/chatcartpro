# Testimonial Request — WhatsApp Message Template

Send this directly to your pilot customer(s) on WhatsApp once they've had ChatCart Pro AI running for at least a week or two (enough time to have a real result to point to).

---

## Message (short version — send this first)

```
Hi [Name]! 👋 Hope ChatCart Pro AI has been useful for [Business Name] so far.

Quick favour — would you be open to sharing a 1-2 line review of your experience? Just a couple of sentences on what's changed for you (faster replies, fewer missed messages, less time on WhatsApp, etc.) would really help other business owners considering it.

Totally fine to say no worries if not — and if yes, I can either write a draft based on what you tell me and you just approve it, or you can send it in your own words, whichever's easier for you.
```

## If they say yes but seem unsure what to write, send this follow-up

```
No pressure to write an essay — even answering these 2 quick questions works:

1. What was the biggest annoyance before (e.g. missed WhatsApp messages, slow replies, no-shows, phone calls piling up)?
2. What's different now with ChatCart Pro AI?

I'll turn it into a short quote and send it back to you to approve before using it anywhere.
```

## Once you have their answer, format it like this before adding to the site

```
"[Their actual words, lightly cleaned up for grammar — do not change the meaning]"
— [Their name], [Their role] at [Business Name]
```

---

## Important — before publishing anywhere

- **Get explicit written confirmation** (a WhatsApp message saying "yes, you can use this" is enough) before putting their name/business on the public site. A screenshot of their approval is good practice to keep on file.
- **Ask if they're comfortable with their real business name being shown**, or whether they'd prefer to stay anonymous (e.g. "Owner, Grocery Store in Dubai" instead of the real name). Some small business owners are cautious about being publicly quoted — respect that.
- If you get a strong quote, consider also asking: **"Would you be open to a 30-second voice note instead of text?"** — a real voice testimonial (even just audio) is far more persuasive than text and you could transcribe it, or even embed the audio player on the site.
- Once you have ONE real, approved testimonial, tell me and I'll swap it into `index.html` in place of the current honest "pilot cohort" placeholder copy — replacing just that one card, not all three, so the page doesn't look sparse if you only have one for now.
