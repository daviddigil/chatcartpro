How these prices were set: Benchmarked against 2026 market rates, generic WhatsApp Business API/CRM tools (Wati, Interakt, Zoko) typically run $40–$500/mo before AI capability is added, and UAE SME WhatsApp chatbots commonly run AED 1,500–5,000/mo. ChatCart Pro AI is priced to sit below that range while including AI order/booking automation, not just messaging, plus multi-modal AI (voice, image, video) as you scale up. AED and INR prices are set directly for each market rather than a straight currency conversion, and are reviewed periodically as Meta's per-message rates and AI model costs change. India-billed accounts are invoiced in INR in line with Meta's 2026 WABA billing requirements.
