Why Businesses Ask For Help

The agent is free to turn on. Getting it to answer well is the hard part.

Meta's AI Business Agent ships with a basic default setup. Left as-is, it often gives vague answers or misses your actual business details.

The setup screens are easy to get wrong on a first try

Business instructions, tone, product info, and escalation settings all need to be filled in deliberately. Skipping or rushing any of them shows up as a bot that gives generic or incorrect answers to real customers.

Your product catalog and details don't connect on their own

For the agent to answer questions about your actual products, pricing, and availability, your catalog needs to be structured and linked correctly, not just described in a paragraph.

Knowing when the AI should hand off to you takes tuning

Left unconfigured, the agent either escalates too much, defeating the point, or not enough, leaving frustrated customers stuck talking to a bot. We tune this to your business.

Most businesses don't have time to test it properly

A good setup means actually chatting with the agent as a customer would, across common questions, before it goes live. We do that testing pass so you don't have to.
