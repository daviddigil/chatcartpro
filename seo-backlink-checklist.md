# SEO Backlink & Directory Checklist — ChatCart Pro AI

Goal: get real, relevant backlinks pointing at chatcartpro.com to build domain authority faster than waiting on organic content alone. Do these roughly in order — general SaaS directories first (fastest, easiest approval), then category-specific ones, then local/regional ones.

Tip: use consistent business info everywhere (same name "ChatCart Pro AI", same description, same logo) — search engines cross-reference this for trust signals (this is called NAP consistency in local SEO).

---

## Tier 1 — General SaaS/startup directories (do these first, ~30-60 min each)

- [ ] **Product Hunt** — producthunt.com — launch as a new product, free, drives real traffic + backlink. Best results if you plan a specific launch day and ask early users to upvote.
- [ ] **SaaSHub** — saashub.com — free listing, good for SaaS-specific search traffic
- [ ] **G2** — g2.com — free to list, reviews matter a lot here later once you have pilot customers willing to review
- [ ] **Capterra** — capterra.com — free listing, strong domain authority
- [ ] **GetApp** — getapp.com — same parent company as Capterra, separate listing
- [ ] **AlternativeTo** — alternativeto.net — you already have competitor-alternative pages built (Wati, Respond.io, SleekFlow, Gupshup) — list ChatCart Pro AI as an alternative to each of these directly on this site too
- [ ] **BetaList** — betalist.com — good if you frame it as still early-stage/pilot phase

## Tier 2 — AI tool directories (high relevance, category is very active right now)

- [ ] **There's An AI For That** (theresanaiforthat.com)
- [ ] **Futurepedia** (futurepedia.io)
- [ ] **AI Tool Hunt** (aitoolhunt.com)
- [ ] **Toolify.ai**
- [ ] **Insidr.ai**

## Tier 3 — WhatsApp/chatbot-specific directories

- [ ] **Chatbots.org** — long-running chatbot directory, still has decent authority
- [ ] Meta's own WhatsApp Business Solution Provider ecosystem pages (if/when you pursue formal BSP partner status — bigger lift, not urgent now)

## Tier 4 — India-specific (matches your DPIIT/Nexsofture registration)

- [ ] **JustDial** — justdial.com — free business listing, huge local search presence in India
- [ ] **IndiaMART** — indiamart.com — B2B focused, good fit for a B2B SaaS
- [ ] **Sulekha** — sulekha.com
- [ ] **Startup India portal** — since Nexsofture is DPIIT-recognized (per the earlier write-up), make sure your Startup India profile links to chatcartpro.com
- [ ] Local city startup community sites/directories (e.g. your specific city's startup association if one exists)

## Tier 5 — UAE-specific

- [ ] **Dubai SME** directory (if eligible/applicable)
- [ ] **Yellow Pages UAE** (yellowpages-uae.com)
- [ ] Local chamber of commerce business directories
- [ ] **Dubai Chamber** member directory (if you pursue membership)

## Tier 6 — Higher-effort, higher-value (do once the above is done)

- [ ] **Guest post or interview** on a small business / SME-focused blog (India or UAE) about WhatsApp automation — genuinely valuable backlink, takes outreach/relationship building
- [ ] **Reddit** — genuine participation (not spam) in r/smallbusiness, r/Entrepreneur, r/SaaS when relevant questions come up about WhatsApp automation or delivery-app commissions — a helpful answer with your link, not a drive-by ad
- [ ] **Indie Hackers** — indiehackers.com — post your build/launch story, community here values transparency about early-stage/pilot status
- [ ] **LinkedIn articles** — publish 1-2 articles under your own profile about the problem you're solving (delivery app commissions, WhatsApp-first commerce in India/UAE) and link to the site

## After submitting

- Track which ones you've done with the checkboxes above (this file lives in the repo, so it's easy to update over time)
- Most directories take a few days to weeks to actually index/approve — don't expect instant traffic
- Once you have 3-5 of these live, check **Google Search Console → Links** report to confirm Google is picking up the new backlinks
