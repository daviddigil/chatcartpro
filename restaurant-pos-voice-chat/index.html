Floor, Kitchen & Delivery

Built for how a restaurant actually runs

Beyond taking the order: the tools your floor, kitchen, and delivery staff use every shift.

Table management

Every table has a live status, available, occupied, or reserved, and every dine-in order is tied to its table from seating through the check.

Kitchen display & ticket tracking

Confirmed orders become kitchen tickets with their own status, new, preparing, ready, served, independent of the customer-facing order status.

Delivery agent dispatch

Delivery orders are assigned to a delivery agent from within the order itself, so dispatch is one click, not a separate phone call.

Receipt & kitchen ticket printing

Printer settings support network, Bluetooth, or USB-connected receipt printers, so tickets and receipts print automatically as orders are confirmed.

Offers & discounts engine

Run a percent or fixed discount across your whole menu, a category, or specific items, on a schedule, applied consistently across every ordering channel.

One reporting view across every channel

See sales, order volume, and channel mix, counter vs. phone vs. WhatsApp, from a single reports view instead of stitching together three systems.
