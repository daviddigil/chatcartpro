The Problem

Small businesses are losing customers and margin every day

Three costs that hit local, small, and micro businesses hardest, and that most conversational AI tools were never built to solve.

Slow response = lost sale

Customers messaging a business on WhatsApp often wait hours for a reply, or never get one. Most walk away to a competitor.

Delivery apps take 20–30%

Grocery and food businesses that rely on aggregator apps give up a huge share of every order just to stay visible.

Hiring staff doesn't scale

A dedicated order-taker or receptionist is a real fixed cost most small and micro businesses simply can't justify.

The Solution

One AI employee, working inside WhatsApp, 24x7

ChatCart Pro AI connects to a business's existing WhatsApp number and automatically handles customer conversations, taking orders and booking appointments end-to-end, with no new app for the business or its customers to install.

Understands intent

Reads natural-language messages and knows whether the customer wants to order, book, or ask a question.

Executes the transaction

Confirms price, checks live stock or calendar availability, and completes the order or booking on the spot.

Follows up automatically

Sends confirmations and reminders without any manual work from the business owner.

Hands off when needed

Escalates complex requests to a human, with full conversation context, so nothing gets missed.
