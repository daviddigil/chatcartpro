Quick answer

Meta's AI Business Agent is a free, native chat tool for WhatsApp and Instagram, configured inside Meta Business Suite and bounded by what that product exposes. ChatCart Pro AI is a custom-built AI employee: we configure real order-taking and appointment booking, sync it to your live inventory and pricing, add voice calling and website chat as connected products, and stay accountable for it working correctly, not just handed back to you after setup.

Where They Actually Differ

Meta's agent answers questions. ChatCart Pro AI runs your front desk.

Both can hold a WhatsApp conversation. The real difference shows up in what happens after "hello."

Real orders, not just answers

Meta AI Business Agent Primarily conversational: shares product info, hours, and FAQs, but isn't built as a transactional order-taking engine.

ChatCart Pro AI Takes the order, checks live stock and pricing, confirms it, and can process payment, all inside the same WhatsApp chat.

Live inventory & pricing sync

Meta AI Business Agent No native real-time inventory integration; answers are only as current as what's manually kept up to date.

ChatCart Pro AI Checks your real-time stock and price list before confirming an order, so customers are never quoted a sold-out item or an old price.

Beyond WhatsApp and Instagram

Meta AI Business Agent Lives entirely inside Meta's own apps, WhatsApp and Instagram chat only.

ChatCart Pro AI Also available as a 24x7 AI voice calling agent for phone calls, and as a chat widget directly on your own website.

Configured for your business, specifically

Meta AI Business Agent Configured through Meta's own setup screens, bounded by whatever options that product exposes.

ChatCart Pro AI We build your order flows, booking logic, and escalation rules around your actual business, not a generic template.

Lead routing to the right person

Meta AI Business Agent Basic handoff exists, but there's no configurable routing to specific sales reps or teams.

ChatCart Pro AI Recognizes buying intent and escalates the conversation straight to the right rep, with delivery and reply tracking.

Who's accountable if it breaks

Meta AI Business Agent Self-serve once configured, Meta operates the infrastructure but isn't a support vendor for your business.

ChatCart Pro AI We build, test, and continue tuning it with you. You have a vendor directly accountable for it working.
